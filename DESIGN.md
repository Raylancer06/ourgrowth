## Brand & Style

This design system embodies pure agricultural freshness, health transparency, and contemporary convenience. Tailored specifically for farm-direct and staple produce commerce—featuring premium ready-to-use staples like "Pure · Peeled · Garlic"—the visual language blends organic authenticity with frictionless modern digital shopping.

The aesthetic fuses **Modern Organic Commerce** with structured, crisp minimalism:
- **Clean Air & Purity:** Generous white space paired with pale leafy tinted backdrops (#F4F7F2) evokes unadulterated freshness, clean packaging, and hygienic farm-to-table delivery.
- **Trust & Vitality:** Deep botanical greens anchor authority and natural origins, energized by vivid sprout-green accents that guide the eye to primary CTAs and deal badges.
- **Approachable Modernity:** Rounded card silhouettes, pill-form micro-tags, cheerful ticker marquee strips, and friendly metric callouts create an open, reassuring shopping experience for families, chefs, and health-conscious food lovers.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immediate connection to lush farm fields, crisp produce, and verified organic purity:

- **Primary (`#1D5D3A`):** Deep Forest Herb green. Used for authoritative section headers, dark contrast background blocks (such as the immersive footer and promotional banners), active pill tags, and key interactive focal points.
- **Secondary (`#70B838` / `#84CC16`):** Vibrant Sprout Lime. Serves as the high-energy conversion driver—applied across "Add to Cart" pill triggers, notification dots, star review highlights, and cheerful ticker dividers.
- **Tertiary (`#0055A5`):** Crisp Growth Blue (derived from brand mark accents). Reserved for corporate trust cues, logistics badges, freshness guarantees, and verified lab-tested badges.
- **Neutral Surface & Base:** Pure stark white (`#FFFFFF`) for elevated product and testimonial cards; subtle tinted mist (`#F4F7F2` / `#EDF3EB`) for section background alternations. Body text is set in soft charcoal (`#2D3832`), avoiding harsh black while preserving crisp contrast.

## Typography

Typography is clean, highly legible, and geometry-driven using **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all roles. This maintains an approachable, contemporary grocery identity that avoids stale corporate rigidity or slow-reading luxury serif styling:

- **Expressive Display & Metrics:** Headings leverage tight tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) and heavy weights (700 and 800) to give impact to food benefit headlines and big numerical counters (e.g., `98%`, `25+`, `100% Pure`).
- **Body & Product Metadata:** Mid-weights and generous line heights guarantee that nutritional statements, weight indicators (`500g`, `1 Kg`), and pricing strike-throughs remain effortlessly scannable across all device densities.
- **Section Eyebrows & Pill Tags:** Set in semi-bold and bold uppercase/title case with subtle expansion (`0.02em`–`0.03em`) for immediate section anchoring.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout operates on a standard 12-column responsive fluid grid with maximum container constraining at `1280px` to maintain optimal focus and photographic balance.

- **Desktop (≥1024px):** 12-column layout with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and 32px to 64px outer page margins. Section vertical cadence maintains generous breathing room (`space-2xl` / 64px–96px) to emulate the open, airy farm aesthetic.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with 20px gutters. Dual-feature cards stack gracefully from 4 columns to 2x2 modular blocks.
- **Mobile (≤767px):** 4-column layout with 12px gutters and 16px page margins. Product listings switch to a tight 2-column or horizontal scroll preview, while complex comparison sections and statistic grids collapse into stacked vertical cards.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth combines tactile softness with clean organic brightness, strictly rejecting heavy drop-shadows or stark dark outlines:

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - Base layer: Warm tinted white canvas (`#FFFFFF` to `#F4F7F2`).
  - Card layer: Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) with a featherweight boundary outline (`1px solid rgba(29, 93, 58, 0.08)`).
- **Subtle Organic Shadows:**
  - **Flat/Resting:** `0px 2px 8px -2px rgba(29, 93, 58, 0.05)`.
  - **Elevated/Hover Cards:** `0px 12px 24px -6px rgba(29, 93, 58, 0.09)`. The subtle forest green tint in the shadow replaces sterile black shadows, creating natural harmony with organic produce photography.
  - **Floating Pills & Floating Badges:** `0px 4px 14px 0px rgba(112, 184, 56, 0.22)`.

## Shapes

The design system embraces welcoming, organic roundedness with an emphasis on soft curves and complete pills:

- **Product & Content Cards:** Constructed with a generous `1.25rem` to `1.5rem` (rounded-2xl) radius, giving packaged garlic and fresh ingredient photography an inviting, safe, premium feel.
- **Badge Indicators, Chips, and Primary Buttons:** Fully rounded pill shapes (`9999px`) provide ergonomic touch targets and friendly framing for status tags (e.g., `✦ Naturally Grown`, `Pure Garlic`, `Shop Now →`).
- **Feature & Media Framing:** Corner rounding on imagery and dual-column graphic banners scales up to `2rem` (rounded-3xl), occasionally paired with soft organic circular icon discs (`rounded-full`).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Cart & Checkout):** Pill-shaped, bright leaf green background (`#70B838`), crisp white text, bold weight. Features smooth scale-up micro-interactions (`transform: translateY(-1px)`) on hover with vibrant ambient green glow.
- **Secondary / Ghost Action:** Outlined forest green (`#1D5D3A`) border with transparent fill, or translucent green wash (`rgba(29, 93, 58, 0.08)`) with dark green text.
- **Icon / Stepper Buttons:** Circular pill buttons for quantity adjusters (`-` / `+`) and video triggers with centered white/green glyphs.

### Chips & Section Eyebrow Badges
- **Pill Eyebrows:** Small pill container with pale mint/lime background (`#EAF5E4`), featuring an organic icon or star prefix (`✦`) followed by deep botanical green text (`#1D5D3A`) in semi-bold tracking.
- **Filter Tags:** Toggleable rounded-full chips; unselected in crisp white with subtle gray-green borders, selected in deep forest green with contrasting white text.

### E-Commerce Product Cards
- **Structure:** Clean white surface (`#FFFFFF`) with `1.25rem` rounded corners, soft green-tinted border, and top-centered isolated packaging/produce photography on crisp transparent or light neutral background.
- **Content Hierarchy:** Category subtitle in muted olive (`#6E8276`), bold product name (`#1D5D3A`), star review cluster in gold-lime, crisp price row showing active price alongside strike-through original value or unit measure (`/ 500g`), completed with a full-width or floating pill "Add to Cart" button.

### Marquee / Ticker Ribbon
- Continuous horizontal animation strip saturated with vibrant sprout lime (`#70B838`) or forest green (`#1D5D3A`). White or contrasting dark text punctuated by geometric starbursts (`✦`) displaying core brand promises (*Everyday Nourishment ✦ 100% Pure Peeled Garlic ✦ Naturally Farm-Fresh ✦ Ethical & Sustainable*).

### Feature & Statistic Blocks
- Two-column cards with high-contrast statistic counters (e.g., `98%`, `25+`) housed inside dedicated soft-tinted or lime-accented accent containers. Paired alongside concise bullet checklists with custom green checkmark badges.

### Testimonial Cards
- Elevated white cards with rounded-2xl geometry. Feature an oversized circular or rounded-square quote badge (`“`) in vibrant lime or forest green at the top left, filled star ratings, genuine customer reviews, and avatar attribution tags.

### Inputs & Form Fields
- Soft, pill-rounded or generous 12px rounded input containers with pale off-white fill (`#F9FAF8`), clear inner padding, and active focus rings colored in sprout lime (`#70B838`) with soft green box-shadow spread.

### Footer
- High-contrast deep organic forest green (`#1D5D3A`) background spanning full canvas width. Multi-column grid containing brand emblem, quick navigation links, certified quality badges, newsletter subscription input, and subtle olive-tinted copyright divider.
